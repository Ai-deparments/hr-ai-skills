# Base interview questions (fallback)

Use these when vacancy-specific questions can't be generated.

1. Tell me about your most recent job: what did you do day to day?
2. Describe a difficult situation with a customer or colleague and what you did.
3. Which skills from this vacancy do you already have? Give an example for one of them.
4. This position works on a {{schedule}} schedule. Does that suit you? What would make it hard?
5. Why do you want this job, and where do you see yourself in a year?

## Role packs (examples)

**Pharmacist:** diploma and certificate validity; pharmacy experience; generic substitutes; prescription-only dispensing; cash register; readiness for 24h and night shifts.
**Cashier:** cash discipline; handling shortages; peak-hour queues.
**Courier:** delivery area knowledge; own transport; handling cold-chain items.
